# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Fastindex welcoming and harassment-free
for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing the actual impact of words and actions.
- Welcome questions and differing technical opinions.
- Keep reviews focused on the work, evidence, and tradeoffs.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, sustained
disruption, and other conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe are not
acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when conduct violates these expectations. Decisions
will consider context, impact, and the need to protect the community.

For conduct concerns, contact the maintainer privately using the contact options on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/manojbajaj95). Do not open a public issue
for a sensitive report. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
